# Today

The Workbench opens on **Today**: your Projects and Practice progress (read from `.datapass/progress.json` in the
workspace), the next suggested step, the runtime's state, and every lab in two families:

- **Learn**: Practice, Mosaic, SparkLab, Airflow, Pipeline, BI and Cloud Lab;
- **Real work**: Projects and the labs where you do tickets with real tools (dbt, Terminal, Infra, and more).
